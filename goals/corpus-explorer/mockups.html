How does the pipeline handle named entity recognition?

24 passages from 6 documents · hybrid retrieval · answered by NotebookLM

Answer

Entity recognition runs through ruby-spacy, and each entity is then enriched with WordNet synsets via rwordnet 1. Entities are protected from clause splitting, so a name never straddles two chunks 3.
