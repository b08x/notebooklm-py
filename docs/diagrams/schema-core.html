Source: db/models.py. Deleting a chunk_runs row (accent header) cascades to its parents, clauses and, through clauses, embeddings. clauses.run_id and parent_id are nullable; clauses.topic_id and fact_checks are on the annotations diagram. local_assets, corpus_notebooks, removal_log and archived_notebooks have no foreign keys; they are keyed to the data above by notebook_id and document_id strings.
